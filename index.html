The term
The term

Where a child grows up shapes how their life turns out. Michaelmas showed why such differences persist: amenities and disadvantages are fixed in place, land and housing prices adjust to them, and households sort by what they can pay. The Lent term asks which parts of the resulting gaps can be changed, by whom and by how much, through studies in which an intervention or a natural experiment moved an outcome.
